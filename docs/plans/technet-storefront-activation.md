## Store and orders

Sell six products: DIY kits and assembled systems for Full TechNet Hub, PisoWiFi only, and PisoPhone only. DIY kits contain prepared, paired parts for customer assembly. Complete systems arrive assembled and tested. Include a prepared router for Full Hub and PisoWiFi. Offer a router as an optional priced add-on for both PisoPhone-only products. Offer a rental phone as an optional paid add-on for assembled Full Hub and PisoPhone systems. Show contents, PHP prices, add-on subtotal, and preparation lead time before ordering. Accept made-to-order purchases. The seller will provide product photos and catalog details.

Require sign-in to order. Offer shipping and local pickup. For shipping, collect the delivery address and let the seller enter a quote before payment. Email the buyer a link to the signed-in order page with the final amount, order reference, and GCash, Maya, and bank transfer instructions. Collect the payment transaction reference and require seller verification before fulfillment. Give the seller a private area to manage products, quote delivery, verify payments, record direct sales, and handle ownership and hardware changes.

## Workshop and first activation

Extend the technician setup program to configure the ordered edition and defaults, pair the assigned router and ESP32 for Full Hub and PisoWiFi, and test the coin acceptor and a paid session for each included product. Clear test data before shipment. Register one kit record per unit for both website and direct sales. Match the edition, licensed components, and printed claim card at handover. Each kit includes one entitlement for one installation. An additional working installation requires another kit or complete system.

The operator powers the kit and connects it to the internet, opens the website on their phone, signs in, and enters the one-time code packed with the kit. Enable redemption at fulfillment. Redemption claims the unit. Activation completes when authenticated hardware verifies and saves its grant. The claiming operator can differ from the buyer. Keep order and payment details visible only to the buyer and authorized staff. Handle concurrent code redemption atomically. A repeat visit by the owner resumes setup.

Full Hub and PisoWiFi bind one router and ESP32 pair and check in through the router. PisoPhone-only binds one ESP32 and checks in directly from that board. Provide phone-accessible local setup to join or change compatible shop Wi-Fi. Issue a signed permanent entitlement bound to the kit, assigned hardware, and edition. Verify and persist it locally before allowing paid operation. Show whether the site accepted the code, is waiting for hardware, is waiting for first phone enrollment, or has confirmed readiness. Use the existing local QR enrollment flow for Full Hub and PisoPhone, and confirm the first rental phone is paired and online. For PisoWiFi and Full Hub, verify paid Wi-Fi and the coin box. For PisoPhone-only, changing the optional router or shop Wi-Fi requires no license reassignment. Verify local rental-phone connectivity on that network. Keep daily administration and subsequent enrollment in the local dashboard.

## Repair, ownership, and license enforcement

- Gate the official browser flasher and new-kit installation images behind authenticated kit ownership and current hardware assignment. On a supported laptop browser, identify the connected ESP32 before the first write. Refuse unrelated boards. Verify device-side license enforcement independently of the website check.
- Open the specific unit from the claiming operator's order or My Systems. For gifts only the claimed operator can order its replacement and see their own repair order. Offer Reflash this ESP32 and Order a replacement ESP32. Paid replacements use the quote and verified-payment flow. Assign the pre-flashed replacement to the unit before shipping or warranty handover. Allow the operator to reflash the original or install the assigned replacement from their account, including when the previous board is dead.
- For Full Hub and PisoWiFi supply replacement routers with OpenWrt and a TechNet setup service already installed. Assign the router to the kit before shipping. The operator connects it and completes software installation and pairing from a phone browser.
- Restore the edition and purchased phone slots on replacement. Record paid slots against the kit at issuance so recovery works with dead hardware. Keep two included slots for phone editions and the existing limit of twelve. Operators configure shop settings and pair phones again after complete loss.
- Let an activated kit run indefinitely during licensing outages. Disable a replaced device's paid service when it next reaches licensing. A replaced device can continue operating while offline.
- Verify disputed or missing codes against fulfillment records. Invalidate the disputed code and audit seller corrections. Require seller approval to transfer a kit, its paid slots, and repair access to a resale buyer. Retire a fully refunded kit's entitlement and repair access after the returned hardware is received. Staff handle edition upgrades.
- Preserve legacy installation and update paths for existing shops. Keep new-kit installation images private. Verify that legacy recovery images cannot bypass new-kit licensing on the shipping hardware.

### Proposed records and invariants

- **Catalog and order lines.** Identify edition and assembly option independently. Validate rental-phone eligibility on the server. Store quantities, agreed unit prices, add-ons and currency on the order so editing a listing cannot change an existing order. Use integer centavos or exact decimal money arithmetic.
- **Order, fulfillment and payment.** Track buyer or staff-recorded direct sale, delivery method, address where needed, shipping quote, final amount, reference, seller verification and fulfillment. Seller-entered direct sales must support recipients whose account does not yet exist. Restrict customer edits after verification. Handle quote-email retry separately from updating the price.
- **Kit and claim.** Give every physical installation a stable kit identifier independent of replaceable hardware. A quantity of three creates three separately claimable units. Generate unpredictable single-use claim codes, store a verifier, and protect printed codes through handover. Redeem in one transaction. Two accounts racing for a code must yield exactly one owner. The owner's interrupted setup resumes from the kit page.
- **Hardware assignment.** Record each licensed component's MAC, workshop-provisioned identity, permanent system ID, assignment history and status. At a fixed licensing endpoint verify the credential and return only the device's own signed authorization. Deny unfamiliar hardware without disturbing the legitimate assignment. Reject duplicate active assignments. For paired editions, replacing the router must preserve the coin box assignment and vice versa. Keep the replacement authorization tied to the selected kit even if the operator owns several.
- **Edition and slots.** Keep a stable ledger of purchased rights for the kit plus a device-verifiable grant for current hardware. Credit each verified slot sale once across system-page orders and seller-recorded direct sales. Sign the system ID, edition, current slot total, hardware binding and entitlement revision. On replacement, reissue valid box-bound slot licenses or an equivalent verified grant derived from the kit ledger. Replaying a request cannot grant extra capacity. Preserve two included phones, at most twelve total, on phone-capable editions.
- **Private artifacts.** Resolve eligible image, chip, edition, version, length, digest and signing metadata from trusted release records. Authorize the current operator and assignment before serving a protected image. Keep legacy public links usable. Check all release destinations for accidental copies of new installation images.

### Operator journeys and failure handling

1. **Order to handover.** Display a PHP subtotal and shipping-pending status before a shipped order is submitted. After the seller quotes shipping, show the exact amount, destination details and order reference on the authenticated order page. For pickup, show the configured pickup details and applicable total. A submitted transaction reference awaits manual verification. An email delivery failure leaves the quote available on the order page and allows notification retry. Do not ship based solely on customer input.
2. **First boot and claim.** Allow the operator's browser and kit to use different internet connections. Match the claim and authenticated kit check-in in either arrival order. Record ownership when the code is redeemed even if the kit is unplugged. Report waiting for kit until it checks in. Release the edition grant only to the current assignment. Interrupted operations resume without a second claim code.
3. **Edition readiness.** For PisoWiFi, confirm the paired coin box and paid Wi-Fi service. For PisoPhone, verify local connectivity over the chosen Wi-Fi network, hand off to local QR enrollment and verify a paired rental phone is online. Full Hub requires both checks. Show instructions for joining the kit network when local access is required. A stale health report, accepted claim, or successful firmware download alone cannot establish readiness.
4. **ESP32 repair.** For a pre-flashed replacement, keep the assignment pending through shipping. From a phone, activate the assigned board and verify pairing with the surviving router where required. Atomically switch the assignment and retire the previous binding. A retry after a lost response recovers the same result. Confirm that the device saved the grant before reporting readiness. For USB restoration, sign in on a supported laptop, select the kit and connect the board by USB. Read identity and chip family before erase or write, authorize the current assignment, verify the image and flash, then verify reboot, identity, entitlement and pairing. Show distinct recovery instructions for unsupported browser, permission denied, disconnected cable, wrong board, incompatible image and failed post-flash verification. Recheck identity if the operator reconnects or switches ports.
5. **Router repair.** For PisoPhone-only guide the operator through changing Wi-Fi and checking rental-phone connectivity. For the router-bound editions the seller registers the prepared replacement and installs the setup service before shipment. The operator plugs in WAN and power and starts recovery from their phone. Use an authenticated outbound connection for the assigned router. Account for service restarts and address changes during configuration. Resume pairing with the surviving ESP32 and report readiness after the edition checks pass. Validate recovery on a phone without SSH, USB, or a second computer.
6. **Offline operation and replacement.** A newly unclaimed kit waits for first online activation. A verified activated installation must survive restart and an extended licensing outage. Replacement recovery can proceed with the old device completely dead. Once a superseded unit reaches licensing, it must persist the disabled state across reboot. Document the accepted possibility that an old unit remains operational while offline.
7. **Transfers and returns.** On approved resale, move operator access, edition and slot rights together and remove the previous operator's repair access. Preserve original buyer privacy. Disputed codes require seller review. A returned and fully refunded unit loses its entitlement and access after receipt. Keep traceability when preparing it for a future purchaser.
