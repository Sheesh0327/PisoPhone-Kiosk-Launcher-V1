# Replan 2026-10-22 (after feature 005, the spinning wheel; raised by an owner error report)

## What was learned
- **005 is released** (router 1.5.14 at 100%, wheel off by default). All 8 criteria passed on the computer; hardware check RS34 and the legal review of the warning text are open.
- **Owner report:** "the PisoWiFi page does not have a play spin wheel button even after turning it on in settings." Reproduced with the test harness (scratchpad script, wheel on the way the console does it): a device that has not paid gets `wh = false` and `gm = -1`, so with the wheel on a visitor sees **neither the wheel nor the old mini-game**. The owner confirmed the phone had paid nothing. Not a code failure: it is what 005's constraint 6 says ("before payment shows no wheel"), but nothing tells the owner or the visitor why, and the old mini-game button was visible to everyone.
- Second, smaller gap found while reproducing: the screen right after paying ("You are online", the `final` view) has no wheel button either; it appears only on the next screen. The mini-game never had one there.
- A requirement I wrote into the spec was taken as given; the owner's own decision 2 for 005 ("the odds panel is always shown") already pointed the other way. Lesson: when a spec hides a feature from some visitors, ask the owner what those visitors should see, not only what they should not.

## What changes
- New feature **006 wheel button for everyone** (`specs/features/006-wheel-button/`): with the wheel on, every visitor sees "Spin the wheel" (also on the "You are online" screen); the wheel page shows the odds to everyone and says "Buy Wi-Fi time first" with Spin disabled when the device has no paid time. Spinning still needs paid time, enforced by the router as before. Router release 1.5.15. Owner chose this on 2026-10-22.
- 005's constraint 6 gets a one-line amendment pointing to 006.
- Ledger: the report is logged (rule 6). `STATUS.md`: 006 added as planned, waiting for approval.
- Roadmap, techstack, mission, constitution: unchanged. Workflow: add to the `sdd-plan` question list "what does a visitor without the precondition (no payment, wheel off, no time) see?".
