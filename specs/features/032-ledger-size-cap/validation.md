## Money and time
Checked, not just asserted: the coin path, pricing, settling, vouchers and `append_row` are untouched. The prune runs only after the new sale row is written, and a failed prune is swallowed (`f032_g3_a_prune_that_fails_never_stops_the_sale_row`). Credit and totals: criterion 2 above, two ways. Rust: the lifetime total read across all files and from `revenue.csv` alone is the same number before and after a prune and equals the sum of every row written. End to end: the real program reconciles against the fake coin box at 135000 = 135000 before and 135002 = 135002 after, the extra 2 being only the sale. Deleting is ordered record, flush, delete; a segment is only deleted when the next file's carry row names its last hash and total. Mutation checks (wrong total in the record, a whole-file read, newest-first deletion) each made tests fail, then were reverted.
