## Money and time
The coin path is not rewritten. What touches it: `ledger::append` first tries the roll, and a roll that fails for any reason never stops the sale row from being written (`let _ = ...`); the row text and hash chain of every sale are unchanged. The soak (70 kills including power cuts, a hung portal, a restart) credits 426 of 430 pesos exactly once and 4 are stranded by kills before the router wrote its window record: the same finding as feature 014 (AO-11, feature 021), not new. `maintenance()` moves `recover()` to its own thread, one at a time (`try_lock`); a customer starting a window still settles first. The monitor only restarts the service; it never touches a payment.
