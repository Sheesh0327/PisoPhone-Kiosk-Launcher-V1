Add a phone

Turns a factory-reset Android phone into a locked rental kiosk that finds its coin box by itself.

You need: a shop that is already set up, the rental phone factory reset and charged, and your own phone or a laptop with internet.

  1. Get the link for the slot. The setup program's last page has it; or open the coin box's page (http://10.0.0.10) and under Tools, Phone slots choose Set up a phone. The link already holds this box's key: you never see it.
  2. On the rental phone's first welcome screen tap the same spot 6 times. A QR reader opens.
  3. On your own phone or laptop open the link, tap Show the setup code and scan it with the rental phone. It joins the Wi-Fi, installs PisoPhone and locks itself.
  4. When it says One last step, do what it says. On most phones: Open the setting and switch on Allow display over other apps. On an Android Go phone: plug it into a computer, tap Allow on the USB debugging question and use Finish over USB on the link's page (Chrome or Edge).
  5. Pair the slot on the coin box's page. The phone is now a kiosk.
No QR reader after 6 taps, or the link says to open it from the coin box

The link must come from the coin box or the setup program; opened on its own the page has nothing to set up. Some phones show the reader on a different welcome screen: go back one screen and tap again. The USB cable tab on the link's page is the fallback.

Take PisoPhone off a phone

On the link's page choose Remove from a phone (USB debugging must be on), or use a recovery code from the setup program's Owner tools.
