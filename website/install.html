Install PisoPhone Kiosk

Follow the interactive guide below to prepare and lock down your Android phone into an automated coin kiosk in minutes.

Step 1 of 5
1

Enable USB Debugging First

Prerequisite for ADB Reboot & Commands
Initial Setup

Before your computer can communicate with the phone or send the automatic recovery reboot command, USB Debugging must be active:

1
Open Settings > About Phone.
2
Find Build Number (or MIUI / Software version) and tap it 7 times rapidly until unlocked.
3
Go to System > Developer Options and toggle USB Debugging to ON.
Cable Check: Use a high-quality USB Data Cable to connect your phone to the PC now. When prompted on the phone, tap "Always Allow".
2

Factory Reset the Device

Clean slate for Device Owner mode
Required

To lock down the phone completely as a dedicated commercial kiosk, Android requires a fresh factory state without any personal accounts or lockscreen credentials.

● Option A (Automatic): With USB Debugging enabled, click below to boot directly into Android Recovery Mode.
● Option B (Manual): Open Settings > System > Reset options > Erase all data (factory reset).
Uses ADB recovery command
3

Setup Wizard: Skip All Accounts

Do not register accounts
Crucial

When the phone restarts and reaches the Android Welcome setup screen, follow these strict rules to allow Device Owner lockdown:

✔ CONNECT WI-FI: Connect to your local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot.
✖ SKIP GOOGLE SIGN-IN: Tap "Skip" or "Don't Copy". Do not sign in!
✖ SKIP OEM ACCOUNT: Skip Samsung, Xiaomi, or manufacturer cloud accounts.
✖ NO LOCK SCREEN: Skip setting any PIN, password, pattern, or fingerprint.
Why? Android strictly forbids provisioning a Device Owner kiosk if user accounts are already registered.
4

Re-enable USB Debugging

Enable on the freshly reset device
Bridge Access

Since factory reset restores default settings, re-enable developer mode on the clean home screen:

1
Open Settings > About Phone.
2
Tap Build Number 7 times rapidly until Developer Mode is unlocked.
3
Go to System > Developer Options, and toggle USB Debugging to ON.
Keep phone connected via USB Data Cable. When prompted on the phone, tap "Always Allow".
