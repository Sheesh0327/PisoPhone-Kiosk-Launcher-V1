Voucher codes

A customer who chooses "Get a voucher code" when paying gets one code worth the minutes the coins bought, valid for 30 days. It works once. The money is in your sales as soon as the coins are counted; using the code adds no income. Codes are not kept on the router, only a fingerprint. You can cancel a code that was not used.

Settings

Takes effect at once. A payment already started keeps the choice it began with. The same setting on the router: piso-setup voucher-choice on|off. When the sales record is bigger than its space (256 to 16384 KB, 1024 by default), the router deletes the oldest months at the start of a month, a few at a time. Your lifetime total, each deleted month's total and the check against the coin box stay; only the line-by-line detail of those months is gone. The space wins: a busy shop may keep fewer months than it would like.

Prices

Pesos to minutes. The best mix of lines is used for any amount, so ₱17 can be 10 + 5 + 1 + 1. Each plan needs a 1-peso line, so every coin counts. A payment already started keeps the prices it began with.
