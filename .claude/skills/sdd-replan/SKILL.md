---
name: sdd-replan
description: Pause between features to update the constitution, roadmap and workflow. Use after a feature is merged or when the owner asks to replan.
---
1. Read `specs/REPLAN.md` and `specs/STATUS.md`.
2. Walk the checklist in `REPLAN.md`. Propose edits to `roadmap.md`, `techstack.md`, `mission.md` and the workflow; a change to `constitution.md` needs a new version number and the owner's yes.
3. Write `specs/replans/YYYY-MM-DD.md`: what was learned, what changes, what is next. Keep it under one page.
4. Update `specs/STATUS.md` and pick the next feature with the owner.
