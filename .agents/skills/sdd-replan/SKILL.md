---
name: sdd-replan
description: Pause between features to update the constitution, roadmap, and workflow. Use after a feature merges or when the owner requests a replan.
---
1. Read `specs/REPLAN.md` and `specs/STATUS.md`.
2. Follow the checklist in `specs/REPLAN.md`. Propose updates to `roadmap.md`, `techstack.md`, `mission.md`, and workflow documentation. Note: any modification to `constitution.md` requires a version increment and explicit owner approval.
3. Author `specs/replans/YYYY-MM-DD.md` summarizing lessons learned, what changed, and upcoming focus areas (under one page).
4. Update `specs/STATUS.md` and select the next prioritized backlog item in alignment with the owner.
